A printed-tee label named after the thing most of us mutter at least once a day. It is self-deprecating, not an insult aimed outward — the joke has always been on us, collectively, affectionately.

The name

Why call it that

Because everyone already says it. In traffic, in a queue at the bank, at 2 a.m. when the electricity goes and comes back three times. It is the national sigh.

We are not printing an insult at anyone. The whole brand is built on the affectionate version — the one you use about yourself and your friends and the country you have no intention of leaving. Every design in the catalogue passes one test: would you wear it in front of your mother? Some of them, honestly, no. That is the point of the other hundred.

What we will not print: anything targeting a caste, religion, region or individual; any political party's name, colour or symbol; and the national flag on fabric.

Production

How a tee
gets made

  • Fabric booked in Tirupur

    100% combed cotton knit, bio-washed and pre-shrunk. 240 GSM for oversized, 200 for crop, 180 for regular, 160 for kids. Booked eight weeks ahead of a drop.

  • Cut and stitched

    Drop-shoulder panels are cut separately from regular ones — an oversized M is not a regular M with more fabric. Neck tape is flat-seamed so kids' tees do not scratch.

  • Printed

    Water-based DTG for catalogue designs, screen print for bulk orders above 150 pieces. No plastisol, no plastic hand-feel.

  • Cured and checked

    Heat-cured, then wash-tested on a sample from every batch. If the print cracks at 20 washes the batch does not ship.

  • Packed and dispatched

    Recycled mailers from Noida. 24–48 hour dispatch, 2–4 days to metros, 4–7 days everywhere else.

Wash care

The short version

Cold machine wash, inside out, similar colours. No bleach. Tumble dry low or line dry in shade. Do not iron directly on the print. Do not dry clean.

Why inside out matters

DTG ink sits in the fibre, but abrasion against a drum still wears the surface. Turning the tee inside out roughly doubles print life. This is the single highest-impact thing you can do.

First wash

Wash separately the first time. Deep colours — mirchi red, maroon, indigo — release a little excess dye on wash one. That is normal and stops after the first cycle.

Shipping & returns

Delivery times

Dispatch in 24–48 hours from Noida. Metro cities 2–4 working days; rest of India 4–7. Free shipping over ₹999, otherwise ₹79. COD available across 19,000+ pin codes at ₹49 extra.

Returns

7 days from delivery, unworn, unwashed, tags on. Free reverse pickup in serviceable pin codes. Refund to source in 5–7 working days after quality check. Size exchanges are free once per order.

What is not returnable

Custom and personalised prints, and bulk orders above 25 units, unless there is a manufacturing defect — in which case we replace the whole batch.
