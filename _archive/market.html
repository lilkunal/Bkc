Published estimates for the same market differ by more than 3×. That is a definitional problem, not a data problem — and it matters if you are planning against it.

How we read it: the honest planning number for a consumer graphic-tee label is the narrow one — roughly $180–250M of genuinely design-led, made-to-order product — sitting inside a much larger $570M+ printing industry that is mostly corporate uniforms, event merch and promotional bulk. Anyone quoting the big number to justify a D2C valuation is counting factory orders as consumer demand.

What we took from them

  • ▸ Beyoung's combos. Bundling is how a sub-₹1,000 category absorbs ₹60–₹80 of shipping.
  • ▸ Bewakoof's social proof placement. Trust signals before product, not after.
  • ▸ Myntra's filter depth. Fit × colour × occasion × audience, shareable via URL.
  • ▸ Amazon's delivery clarity. A date beats a promise.

What we refused to copy

  • ▸ Permanent 60% off. Trains customers to wait and devalues the product.
  • ▸ Men-only merchandising. A merchandising choice sold as a market truth.
  • ▸ Licensed character IP. Renting someone else's brand at a royalty.
  • ▸ SKU sprawl. Marketplaces win on selection; a label wins on point of view.

Add a 12–18% return rate and 2% payment-gateway fees and the break-even shifts up by roughly ₹90–₹120 a unit. That is the entire argument for the oversized cut: fewer size-related returns on a garment that is deliberately not fitted. Our floor price is ₹499 on kids and ₹649 on regular fits — and we do not discount below landed cost to buy revenue.

05 — Position

Where BKC
actually sits

Strengths

  • A brand name nobody can politely ignore — organic recall at zero media spend
  • Occasion coverage no competitor systematically works: 23 drops including regional festivals
  • Animal and insect capsules with a genuine 2% shelter commitment
  • Women, kids and pride built into primary navigation, not a sub-menu
  • Design-led, no licensing royalties leaving the P&L

Weaknesses

  • The name itself blocks mainstream marketplace listings and most ad platforms
  • No offline presence against Beyoung's planned 300 stores
  • Single-category business — no bottomwear, no accessories to lift AOV
  • Print-on-demand margins are structurally thinner than screen-print at volume

Opportunities

  • Regional festivals (Onam, Pongal, Chhath, Bihu) — near-zero national competition
  • Civic occasions: nobody is printing non-partisan turnout merchandise at retail
  • Bulk B2B — baraat squads, college fests, corporate offsites at screen-print margins
  • Quick-commerce apparel is opening up; festival tees are an impulse-timing product
  • Tier-2/3 growth where Beyoung has proven the demand exists

Threats

  • Amazon's unbranded 240 GSM blanks at ₹399 resetting price expectations
  • Design theft — a slogan tee is copied within 72 hours
  • Discount warfare from AJIO and Myntra during festive sales
  • Cotton price volatility hitting a fixed retail price point
  • Reputational risk: one badly judged civic design undoes the non-partisan position

06 — The plan

Five bets

  • Own the calendar, not the category

    Nobody wins "graphic tees" outright. Winning fourteen days around Onam, or the week of a state poll, is achievable — and it repeats every year. Production is planned eight weeks ahead of each drop.

  • Sell the fit, not just the print

    Oversized versus regular is the highest-intent filter on the site. Every artwork ships in four cuts, and the product page switches silhouette live so the customer never guesses.

  • Refuse the discount ladder

    MRP stays honest. At ₹899 we hold ~44% gross margin; at ₹599 the business is charity. Bundles lift AOV instead — the one lesson worth taking wholesale from Beyoung.

  • Serve the audiences the incumbents treat as an afterthought

    If ~90% of a leading competitor's revenue is male, that is a structural opening. Women's cuts, kids 2–13Y and a year-round pride range are core catalogue, not a June campaign.

  • Stay non-partisan, permanently

    No party names, colours or symbols; no national flag on fabric. It costs us some designs and it makes the civic range durable rather than seasonal.
