Voting and protest, without a side

We print for polling day and for peaceful assembly, and we hold a hard line while doing it: no party names, no party colours, no party symbols, and never the national flag on fabric. Our civic tees argue for the mechanism — turn up, ask questions, read the constitution, do it peacefully — not for an outcome. That is what makes them safe to wear anywhere, including inside a polling station.
